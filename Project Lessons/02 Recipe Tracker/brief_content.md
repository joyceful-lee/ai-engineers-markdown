## Meet Your Client

**River loves trying new recipes, but finding ones that use the ingredients she has takes longer than cooking does!** She always finds something that looks delicious, only to find that she needs another trip to the store. **What do we do?**

Creating a recipe collection gives River somewhere to look, but she still needs help finding meals that fit her ingredients and schedule.

```talker
{
name: River
bubble: speech

I want to enter what I have and see a few recipes I can make.
```

A **recommendation system** helps people choose by suggesting options that match their needs and preferences. For River, the site could recommend recipes based on her available ingredients, preparation time, dietary preferences, and meals she has liked before.

## Your Goal

Build **Recipe Tracker**, where users save and share recipes and receive recommendations that help them decide what to cook.

Use GitHub to upload your code, Neon for authentication and database storage, and Vercel for image storage and deployment.

```checkbox
{
title: Project Requirements
color: #e7f7dc
}
- Let users sign up, log in, and log out.
- Support creating, editing, archiving, and deleting recipes with ingredients, measurements, steps, servings, and images.
- Organize recipes into multiple collections with browsing, search, and filters.
- Maintain a shared ingredient library with duplicate checks and recipe-specific measurements.
- Recommend saved recipes using cooking preferences and previous activity, explaining each suggestion.
- Build customizable meal plans and generate shopping lists with accurate quantities.
- Allow meal plans and shopping lists to be shared, copied, and downloaded.
```
