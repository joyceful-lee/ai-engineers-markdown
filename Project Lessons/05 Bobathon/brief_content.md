## Meet Your Client

**Tammy owns Bobathon, a boba shop with more choices than a regular menu can explain.** Customers want creative drinks, but too many choices at once make the ordering process slow and confusing. Tammy wants to help customers order without having to explain everything they want. **What do we do?**

```talker
{
name: Tammy
bubble: speech
face: 🧋
}
Last Tuesday, one customer held up the line for five minutes because they were still trying to understand the menu. I want people to make fun drinks, but I do not want them to panic at the counter.
```

Tammy's menu lists every ingredient, but customers struggle to tell which choices go together and what to choose first.

```talker
{
name: Tammy
bubble: speech
face: 🧋
}
What if we asked one question at a time? Customers could choose a tea, then milk, then ice, then toppings. By the end, they would have made something that feels like their drink.
```

Great idea! This is **progressive disclosure**. Progressive disclosure is when you show people the choices they need at each step and revealing more options as they continue. Instead of showing every ingredient at once, Bobathon can show tea first, then milk, ice, and toppings.

A progress indicator and a small drink preview can help customers understand where they are and what they have chosen. They can still go back and change an earlier choice without starting over.

## Your Goal

Build **Bobathon**, using progressive disclosure to guide customers through tea, milk, ice, and toppings one step at a time. Show the drink taking shape and finish with an order card or code that staff can read.

Use GitHub to upload your code, Neon for menu and order storage, and Vercel for deployment. Customers can build a drink without an account.

```checkbox

- Let users pick tea type and how much tea goes in the cup.
- Let users pick milk type and how much milk goes in the cup.
- Let users choose an ice level for the drink.
- Let users add one or more toppings to the same drink.
- Show a clear drink summary that updates as the user builds their order.
- Finish with an order card or code the user could show a cashier at the register.
```
