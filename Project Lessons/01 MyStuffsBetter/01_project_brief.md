# MyStuffsBetter

Card collections are hard to organize and share with friends who live far away. Our solution? **A collection platform where users build digital binders, share their cards, and follow other collectors.**

```card
{
title: In this project, you will
color: #fff8bf
}
- Listen to, consider the needs of, and design for a client
- Learn the following concepts:
    - User-generated content
    - Content marketplaces
    - Guest accounts
- Build a full-stack site where users create collections, add items, and subscribe to other collections.
- Store collection data in a database and images in Vercel Blob.
- Test your site like a real collector and keep track of every change you make.
- Apply the **user-generated content** and **content marketplace** concepts to create a collection site people want to follow.
```

By the end of this project, you'll have made a working digital binder collection. **Let's get started!**
