## Meet Your Client

**Jamie collects trading cards, but finds it difficult to show off his collection online.** His friends live in different states so they share photos of their cards through messages. Since all his photos are scattered across different conversations, it's impossible to see his complete collection! **What do we do?**

```talker
{
name: Jamie
bubble: speech
face: 🧑
}
Sometimes my friends ask if I have a card I'm pretty sure I shared before, but I still have to search through my binder again and again.
```

Jamie tried using Google Drive to share images before, but he couldn't add information about the card without making it more confusing. A website with a database can organize each card by name, set, or binder and display the collection in one place!

```talker
{
name: Jamie
bubble: speech
face: 🧑
}
I'd love if each collection had its own page! My friends could follow the collections they care about instead of asking me for pictures every week.
```

That's a great idea! Users won't just browse the site, but contribute to making it even better! This is called **user-generated content**.

## Your Goal

Build the website, **MyStuffsBetter**, that will allow users to create collections, add cards, and publish collections. Users will also be able to browse published collections and subscribe to ones they want to keep track of.

You should use GitHub to upload your code, Neon for authentication and database storage, and Vercel for image storage and deployment.

```checkbox
{
title: Project Requirements
color: #e7f7dc
}
- Let users sign up, log in, and log out.
- Allow signed-in users to create, edit, and delete binders for TCGs.
- Add cards to a binder with a name, rarity, and optional image.
- Let owners make binders public or private, with private binders accessible only to their owners.
- Provide a public gallery with search, filtering, and sorting.
- Let signed-in users like and subscribe to public binders.
- Provide personal pages for liked binders, subscriptions, and new-card updates.
```
